Trojan Odyssey

Project Lead · Pixel-Art USC Campus Game

As Project Lead, I took a team of designers and developers from ideation to final build on Trojan Odyssey, a pixel-art game set on the USC campus.

I ran the project in Asana, tracking timelines and progress, and used Aseprite to shape the pixel-art direction for the map and scenes.

I also led branding and marketing, including a physical sticker line.

Project Leadership Aseprite Asana / Agile Workflow Branding & Marketing

Concept & Planning

Early sketch mapping USC into game zones: Frat Row, the Village, Marshall, Roski, Dornsife, and a "boss battle" against UCLA and then-president Carol Folt.

Brainstorm on genre, mechanics, story, and art style, including a fetch quest in an enchanted USC and pixel-art references (Stardew Valley, Celeste, Shovel Knight, Enter the Gungeon, Zelda: Minish Cap).
