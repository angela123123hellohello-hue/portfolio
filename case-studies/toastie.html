Toastie — Wellness Mobile App Design

UI/UX Designer · Jan 2022 · InnoD's CreateSC Design Competition

Designed with teammates Hitomi and Joanne, Toastie tackles modern burnout and the discomfort of oversharing feelings — with close friends and on social media alike. Inspired by the warmth of bread, the app's avatar acts as a confidential friend that guides users through their emotions over voice chat rather than text.

Users fully vent and self-reflect through questions the avatar poses, staying in full control of their privacy — choosing whether to share, delete, or keep each recorded voice message private. Toastie also suggests tailored "wellness tasks," nudging users toward a meditation or physical activity suited to how they're feeling.

The team built the brand around a warm orange-and-yellow palette to embody companionship and comfort, carried through the full UI and prototype.
