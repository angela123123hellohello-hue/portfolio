Haute Magazine — Marketing & Pitch Deck

Marketing Team · Fall 2021 · USC Haute Magazine

I was on the Marketing Team at Haute Magazine, USC's student-run arts, culture, and fashion publication, which has featured Billie Eilish and Rihanna and partnered with Vans.

I built "On The Edge," the sponsorship deck for the Fall 2021 issue on '90s punk and grunge. It covered Haute's brand and reach (450,000+ global views), the issue's creative direction, and the launch party plan: a fashion show, light installation, live concert, and sponsor activations at a 1,000-person venue.
